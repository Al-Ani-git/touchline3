The ball follows your boots. No target dot, no direction snap — your body angle is your aim. Set pieces: move your receiver, then hold a kick button to deliver.

01 / MOVE & FACEWASD / arrows or the left stick. Shift or the stick's outer ring to sprint. V cycles camera; wheel or B zooms.
02 / PLANT & STRIKEHold Space / Shoot to load. Power lifts shots higher and harder. Turn with movement or the mouse. Release to kick. Space also jumps for headers.
03 / BUILD THE PLAYQ / Pass plays along the ground. E / Cross lifts it very high — the longer you hold, the steeper it climbs. Corners land on your selected player.
04 / WIN IT BACKSpace / Tackle slides through the ball and switches you onto the winner. Goal kicks & held saves: move a receiver, then hold Q / E / Space to deliver.
